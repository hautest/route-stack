The layers take the stack's place across, not the viewport's: a stack beside a sidebar slides beside it, where it used to jump to the viewport's left edge and width for the slide. The swipe measures its edge and its distance from the stack's edges too. The sliding screens are not clipped to the stack: what is beside it in the normal flow is slid over, so it has to be drawn over the stack.
